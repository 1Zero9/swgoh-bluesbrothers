Section 01

Mod Anatomy & Slot Primary Rules

Every character equips 6 modification pieces. Left-side mods (Square, Diamond, Circle) have strict primary stat pools, while Right-side mods (Arrow, Triangle, Cross) contain the game's most critical variable stats like Speed, Critical Damage, and Potency / Tenacity.

Square

Transmitter • Slot 1
Static

Primary: Offense % ONLY (+5.88% / +8.5%)

Because the primary is guaranteed, its worth is determined entirely by its Speed secondary and complementary substats.

Arrow

Receiver • Slot 2
Speed King

Allowed Primaries:

Speed (+30 / +32) Offense % Health % Protection % Crit Avoid Defense % Accuracy

90% of characters want a Speed primary. High relic attackers (Drogan, Dark Trooper) can use Offense % arrows if secondary speed rolls are 20+.

Diamond

Processor • Slot 3
Static

Primary: Defense % ONLY (+11.75% / +20%)

Static defense primary. Like the square, sell if no speed substat is uncovered by Tier C (Blue).

Triangle

Holofiber • Slot 4
Rarest Slot

Allowed Primaries:

Crit Damage (+36% / +42%) Crit Chance % Offense % Health % Protection % Defense %

The highest DPS impact slot in SWGOH. Keep all Crit Damage and Offense % triangles with visible Speed.

Circle

Data-Bus • Slot 5
Dual Stat

Allowed Primaries:

Protection % (+23.5% / +24%) Health % (+5.88% / +16%)

Choose Health % for characters who ramp max HP (Grievous, Malak, Padmé, Kelleran Beq). Choose Protection % for shield regenerators.

Cross

Multiplexer • Slot 6
Utility Core

Allowed Primaries:

Potency % (+24% / +30%) Tenacity % (+24% / +35%) Offense % Health % Protection % Defense %

Equip Potency on debuff engines (Vader, Mara Jade, C-3PO) and Tenacity on solo juggernauts (Wampa, Savage, Malak).

Color Tier Upgrades (Slicing)

Each tier corresponds to how many secondary stats start revealed. When upgraded, existing secondaries roll higher values.

Mod Store: What to Buy vs. Skip

The Mod Store refreshes every 6 hours. Use Credits and Ship Building Materials carefully.

Always Buy (Credits / Ship Currency Only)

  • +30 Speed Arrows on Speed, Health, Offense, or Potency sets.
  • Gold (5A) mods showing +5 Speed with at least two complementary % stats (e.g. Offense % & CC, or Health % & Defense %).
  • Crit Damage Triangles (Crit Damage or Offense set) that show +4 or +5 Speed.
  • Potency / Tenacity Crosses matching their set icon with visible +4 or +5 Speed.

Never Buy (Waste of Resources)

  • Never spend Crystals in the Mod Store. Use crystals for energy refreshes instead.
  • Grey (5E) mods in the store: Never gamble on blind substats.
  • Accuracy Arrows & Defense Crosses (hyper-niche with zero speed value).
  • 1-Dot through 4-Dot mods (sub-optimal stats that cannot reach 6-dot).
